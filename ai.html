AI & Enterprise AI

Enterprise AI, designed for operations rather than demonstrations.

We help organizations identify where AI creates real value, design solutions that fit their architecture and guide adoption under clear governance.

Discuss Your AI PrioritiesAI Governance

Overview

From opportunity to operating capability.

Many organizations have experimented with AI. Fewer have moved beyond pilots into dependable, governed operations. The gap is rarely the technology itself; it is prioritization, data readiness, ownership and governance.

Intlex works with leadership and technical teams to build an AI portfolio grounded in business value, then provides the advisory, architecture and adoption support to move the highest-value use cases into production.

Our approach applies equally to organizations running SAP, other enterprise platforms or a combination of both.

Typical outcomes

  • A prioritized, funded AI use-case portfolio
  • A target architecture for enterprise AI
  • Governance and risk controls appropriate to each use case
  • A clear path from pilot to production
  • Adoption plans and success measures

Approach

How we deliver enterprise AI.

  1. Assess

    Understand business priorities, current architecture, data readiness and organizational capacity for change.

  2. Strategize

    Define a prioritized roadmap of use cases and investments with clear value, ownership and sequencing.

  3. Design

    Shape target architecture, solution approach, integration patterns and the operating model to support them.

  4. Govern

    Establish policies, controls, risk management and accountability so adoption remains responsible and sustainable.

  5. Transform

    Guide implementation, measure outcomes and scale what works across functions and regions.
