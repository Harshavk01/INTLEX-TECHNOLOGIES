Overview

AI anchored in core business processes.

For organizations running SAP, the most valuable AI opportunities often sit inside existing processes: financial close, procurement, supply chain planning, maintenance and order management.

Intlex combines SAP process knowledge with enterprise AI expertise. We help clients understand which capabilities are available within the SAP platform, where external AI services add value, and how to integrate both under one governance model.

The result is an SAP AI roadmap that respects existing investments, aligns with S/4HANA programs and delivers measurable process improvements.

Where we help

  • SAP S/4HANA and ECC landscapes
  • SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP)
  • SAP Business AI and embedded capabilities
  • SAP data and analytics environments
  • Integration with non-SAP AI and data platforms
