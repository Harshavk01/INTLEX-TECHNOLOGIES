Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, business email, company, job title, country, phone number and message. We may also collect limited technical information, such as browser type and pages visited, to maintain and improve the website.

How we use information

We use enquiry information to respond to your request, arrange conversations and manage our business relationship with you. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the website and manage enquiries, under appropriate confidentiality and data protection obligations, or where required by law.

Your rights

Depending on where you are located, including under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@intlextech.com.

Cookies

This website uses only cookies necessary for its operation. If analytics or other non-essential cookies are introduced, we will request consent where required and update this policy.

Retention and security

We retain enquiry information only as long as needed for the purposes described and apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@intlextech.com.
